## 3. New surface summary (for traceability)

- **Models:** Conversation, ConversationParticipant, Message, MessageAttachment, MessageRevision, MessageReport (+ReportStatus), MessagingSettings, PendingMessageEmail. Plus User.isSafeguardingLead. (FR-1..4, FR-30, FR-31)
- **Services (`src/server/messaging/`):** contact-rules, contacts, conversations, messages, read-state, notifications/pending-email, quiet-hours, safeguarding, maintenance (`sendDueMessageEmails`). (FR-5..8, FR-14, FR-15, FR-19..27)
- **Routes:** conversations (list/create/get/read/mute/archive/leave), conversation messages (list before/after, send), messages/:id (patch/delete/report), messaging/unread-count, messaging/contacts, me/messaging-settings, safeguarding reports + conversations, and a hook into the internal dispatch route. (FR-28, FR-20)
- **Pages:** `/messages`, `/messages/[conversationId]`, `/admin/safeguarding`, plus a Quiet hours section on `/account/notifications`, student layout actions and roster selection. (FR-9..18, FR-21, FR-23, FR-24)
- **Registries:** 2 notification types, 1 file context, 1 nav item (+badge). (FR-18, FR-19, FR-26, FR-27)
